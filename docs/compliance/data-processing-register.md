## Data minimisation decisions

- Do not use recipient contact details for marketing.
- Do not store full card details in Shore & Grace systems.
- Do not ask customers to place special-category or other sensitive information
  in gift messages or delivery instructions.
- Keep optional browsing history on the device and clear it when personalisation
  consent is withdrawn.
- Anonymise approved review content when an account is deleted if the review is
  retained.
- Retain only the minimum order information required after account closure.

## Review triggers

Review this register before enabling analytics, advertising, a new payment
method, a mailing platform, automated reminders, a new delivery partner or any
new use of purchase history.
